packs/association-fr/ · pk-3d0f53

Four-level AI evaluation, as a routine

expérimental

Runs the four-level AI evaluation framework (model, product, user, impact) of the AI Evaluation Playbook as a routine over a portfolio: a status file per product with its evidence, a readiness gate before each level, an eval-dataset and an engagement-funnel recipe, outcome data joined to the agent version each person used, and a report by level per cluster. It is an operating layer over the playbook and the skills its authors publish, which it imports and never rewrites.

Pour qui · An organization that builds, funds or supports AI products for social outcomes and wants to know, product by product, which level of evidence each one has and what it is ready for next. A funder following a portfolio, or a single nonprofit with one product.

packs/four-levels/ · pk-a4ff18

Nonprofit base

expérimental

The work every nonprofit does around its programmes, as recipes over its own files: funder and donor relations (one file per funder, a verbatim log of every figure sent, talking points, open calls), grant reporting checked against that log, meeting notes into dated journals, communications in the organization's voice with a story bank where every line carries its fact, an events and editorial calendar, and a programme or cohort run from its handbook.

Pour qui · A nonprofit or a funder of any size that raises money, reports to funders, meets partners, communicates, and runs a programme (an accelerator, a fellowship, a cohort). Two people or two hundred; the files are the same.
