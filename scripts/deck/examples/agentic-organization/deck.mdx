{/* A starter for a deck about becoming an agentic organization: the first conversation with a

   node scripts/deck/build.mjs new <slug> --from=agentic-organization
   then replaces the client, keeps only the slides that serve, and is built like any other. This one
   builds with: node scripts/deck/build.mjs build scripts/deck/examples/agentic-organization

   Larkfield Joinery, Fernhill Advisory, the people, the orders and every figure are invented.

   The plan, one line per slide: its title, then what it proves.
   Opening
   1. Cover: what the hour is about, and who is speaking.
   2. The two people you will work with: who we are, in two minutes.
   The client and its stakes (a quarter of an hour at most; the client corrects what we read)
   3. Larkfield and its trades: we have read the company from the outside.
   4. Six sentences heard in every company: the pain, in words the room has heard this week.
   5. Where your teams start from: the room, from its own cards.
   Our support
   6. The agentic organization in one loop: a shared base that learns from each correction.
   7. Four ways of working with AI: levels one and two can be skipped.
   8. Your teams take back their tools: the chain of intermediaries, then the agents.
   9. What your teams see and what runs under it: the complexity is ours to carry.
   10. One source and the views rebuilt from it: every deliverable is a view of the source.
   11. A system that keeps learning: one correction redraws a plan and writes a rule.
   12. A rule base for a joinery: the rules grow as the orders arrive.
   13. A common base and its variants: one base for the company, a variant per site.
   14. A remark becomes a rule and the tracking says so: nothing is lost between review and rule.
   Next steps
   15. How we would start together: four steps, each decided on the last.
   16. The tables for the directors' day: who works on what.
   17. Your rule base installed before the day: what is ready when they arrive.
   18. Closing: the next concrete step. */}
