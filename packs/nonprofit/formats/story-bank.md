# The story bank

`storyBankPath`: the formulations and anecdotes worth saying again, in an interview, a panel, a
pitch, a post. They are found once while preparing a talk, then lost with the prep notes, and the
next person either rebuilds them or says an approximate version.

**An entry never travels alone: it carries the verifiable fact behind it and its source.** A
formulation without its fact becomes, within months, a claim nobody can check, and that is how a
figure said on stage ends up contradicting the published one.

```markdown
## The test set that said no

> Our own evaluation told us not to replace the paper leaflet, and we listened.

- **Serves for**: "how do you avoid a solution looking for a problem?"
- **The fact behind it**: the 2026 pilot compared the chatbot with the printed leaflet on 300 visits;
  comprehension was the same and cost was four times higher (projects/pilot/log.md, 2026-06-12).
- **Source**: projects/pilot/evaluation.md
- **Status**: validated by @sam, 2026-07-01. Said in public at the Example Summit, 2026-09-18, and it landed.
- **Careful**: the comparison covered one district; do not generalise it.
```

## Rules

- An entry comes in the session where someone says "I'm keeping that one", with the fact, the
  source, the date and who validated it. **Proposed** until a person validates it.
- A figure meant for a funder belongs in the talking points, which answer a stricter question.
- A person quoted in an entry knows they are; a critical verbatim stays inside the organization, and
  only the formulation travels.
- A researcher is cited through their published work, with the reference opened before it is cited,
  never through something said in a meeting.
