# Meeting notes

A meeting that leaves nothing in the files did not happen for the next person, and a recap written
from a summary tool's notes alone carries its mistakes. So the transcript is the source when there is
one, and each thing said goes to the file that owns it.

## 1. Clean the transcript first

Before extracting anything: drop the noise of speech (false starts, repetitions), correct the
organization's own vocabulary from a list it keeps (names of programmes, partners, products, with the
transcription errors seen before), and **flag an uncertain term as "to confirm", never smooth it**.
A name or a figure the transcription may have mangled stays visibly uncertain until a person settles
it. When there is no transcript, only notes, say "notes only" on everything written from them.

Notes generated by a meeting tool flatten ranges and confuse speakers: a decision or a figure taken
from them is checked against the transcript passage before it is written anywhere.

## 2. Route what was said

| What came out of it | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| An exchange with a funder, participant or partner | one journal line in their file (`funders`, `formats/participant.md`, or the relationship file the organization uses), newest first: date, what happened, where the notes are |
| A decision about what the organization does or says | `source/decisions.md`, a dated entry with the why |
| Something that happened, worth remembering at year end | one line in `source/journal.md` (`source/formats/journal.md`) |
| An action | the to-do list it belongs to, in the format of `source/formats/todo.md`, with an owner |
| A commitment to a funder | the funder's *What we committed to* |
| A formulation someone wants to keep | the story bank, as **proposed** (`communications`) |
| A fact or figure from outside | the kit's `research` skill, with its source |

The cleaned notes themselves, when worth keeping, go in a `notes/` folder beside the file they
concern, named `YYYY-MM-DD-<who>.md`. Journal lines point to them.

## 3. Show, then write

Show the entries before writing them. Attribute what was said by what the transcript says, never by
whose account recorded the call. A thing said in passing in another meeting is a source too, and the
entry says it was second-hand.

## Recaps for the team

A recap posted to the team follows `source/formats/message.md`: what happened, the decisions, the
actions with owners. Drafted here, posted by a person, on the channel they confirm.
