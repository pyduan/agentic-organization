# decks

Decks made for a customer. One folder per deck, `<slug>/deck.mdx` with `company: <customer slug>` in
its frontmatter, and its images in `<slug>/assets/`. The intranet builds them and serves them under
`/decks/<slug>/`.

The method: `__CENTRAL__/.claude/skills/new-deck/SKILL.md` and `__CENTRAL__/source/formats/deck.md`.
