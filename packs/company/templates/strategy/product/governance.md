## The rule that governs the rest

**The code knows no special case. It reads records.**

What varies from one case to another is a data file, versioned and published separately. The program
is an interpreter of those records, and it is the same everywhere.

The consequence is the one we want: adding a case means publishing one more checked record, with no
software release, and no installation in the field takes a risk because something new was
supported.
