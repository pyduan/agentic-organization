## The sentence

What we do, for whom, and what it replaces. One sentence, the one in `one_liner`. If it needs two,
the positioning is not finished.

## The ideal customer

Described by signals observable from outside, not by adjectives. "More than 200 people and a
management system over ten years old" is a signal, "an ambitious company" is not. For a consumer
target: "owns two bikes and logs every ride online" is a signal, "enthusiast" is not.

## What we do not do

The `icp_disqualifiers`, in prose. As useful as the rest and shorter to write: what is read here saves
a meeting a month.
