# strategy

Product, market, business model, legal, finance. What we sell, to whom, and how we make money from it.

- `product/`: positioning, vision, SWOT, product journal.
- `marketing/`: name, message, channels.
- `legal/`: articles, capital, standard contracts.
- `finance/`: business model, forecast, cash.
- `thesis.md`: the convictions of the moment, written by hand.
- `market.md`: the market and those who already hold it.

**What does not go here:** a decision already taken. It goes in `DECISIONS.md`. This repo holds the
analysis; the decisions file holds the call.
