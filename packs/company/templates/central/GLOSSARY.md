# Glossary

Terms that recur, including customers' vocabulary. A term is here because somebody got it wrong once.
When a term carries a definition from law, tax or a trade, check it against a source before adding it
(`AGENTS.md` ▸ *A term of art the owner uses is an input to check*).

## Ours

**Agentic organization.** The operating model these repos run on: sources of truth as versioned plain
text, everything else regenerated from them by agents.

**Control code.** Code that measures or displays: renderers, comparators, checks, the intranet. We
write it freely. It never carries a business rule.

**Satellite.** A repo attached to the central routing repo, holding one domain.

**ICP, ideal customer profile.** The customer we are built to serve. Defined in
`strategy/product/positioning.md`.

**USP, unique selling proposition.** What we have that the alternatives do not. Same file.

**War room.** An intensive block, days or weeks, with the customer's people and ours in one room, on
one problem.

## Setting the method up at a customer

For a company that sells the agentic organization to its customers. If __COMPANY__ does not, delete
this section with the company pack's `workshop` and `customer-onboarding` skills.

**Rule base.** A customer's business rules, written in their language in markdown, in their own
repos. The asset. It stays with them.

**Unit.** A trade inside a customer with its own rule base and its own repo. At a manufacturer: the
drawing office, order entry, transport, pricing.

**Before-and-after pair.** A file as a tool produced it, and the same file after a person corrected
it. The raw material rules are extracted from. Ten to fifteen pairs is the working minimum.
